About

#Somuchability

Love 21 is a Hong Kong charity. We build programmes around what members can do, not what they cannot.

What we do

We run sport, fitness, dance, and nutrition classes. We also offer family support, including parent counselling. Members teach and lead too: swim coaching, kitchen demos, art, and more.

Love 21 Foundation Limited is a registered charity. We keep the work practical. If it does not help a real family or member, we drop it.
