About Love 21

#Somuchability

A Hong Kong charity empowering the Down syndrome, autistic, and neurodiverse community through sport, nutrition, and holistic support.

Our story

Love 21 Foundation Limited is a registered charity. We take a strengths-based approach so members and families can grow through programmes that feel practical — not performative.

Classes cover sports, fitness, dance, nutrition coaching, and family support including parent counselling. Members also share skills through our talent marketplace: coaching, kitchen demos, art, and more.
