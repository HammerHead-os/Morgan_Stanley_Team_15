Our story

LOVE 21 is a Hong Kong charity dedicated to empowering the Down syndrome and autistic community through sport, nutrition, and holistic support programmes.

Our work began with sport and a simple observation: people thrive when the environment focuses on their strengths. The programme grew to include one-to-one nutrition support, practical cooking, counselling, and parent connection.

The same principle shapes this website. Activities, volunteering, giving, and personal progress are connected so that every visit can lead to a clear next step.
