Our story

Start with what people can do.

Love 21 is a Hong Kong charity dedicated to empowering the Down syndrome and autistic community through sport, nutrition, and holistic support.

We pair practical programmes with family support, visible pathways to participation, and a simple belief: every person brings ability worth recognising.
